Web · Booking platform

Inyamibwa

A booking site for a traditional dance troupe. The old process ran through phone calls and word of mouth, so clients often couldn't tell what the troupe did or when they were free. I designed a site that shows upcoming performances and lets clients request a booking without a phone call.

Mobile · Transit app

Bus tracking app

A mobile app for tracking buses in real time, so riders stop guessing when the next one actually arrives. Alongside tracking, I designed a lost-and-found flow — riders can log an item they've lost or found on a route and match it against the same trip.

Mobile · AR ordering

AR menu

An app for viewing meals in real time before ordering. Instead of a photo on a laminated card, a diner points their camera at the table and sees the dish itself, at scale — what it actually looks like before it arrives.

About

Mutaboba Elois

My work leans toward products with a real operational problem underneath — booking systems, ordering flows, tracking tools — where the interface has to hold up under actual daily use.

I work closely with teams day to day, coordinating design work and feedback through ClickUp so nothing gets lost between a comment and a shipped screen.
